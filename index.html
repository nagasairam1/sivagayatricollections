❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Are your products original gold?

A: No, we specialize in premium imitation jewellery that gives you the look and feel of gold at affordable prices.

Q: Do you offer COD (Cash on Delivery)?

A: Currently, orders are accepted via UPI payment only for secure and faster transactions.

Q: Can I order through WhatsApp?

A: Yes! WhatsApp is our primary order channel. Simply message us at +91 83410 26969 with your requirements.

Q: Do you ship all over India?

A: Yes, we provide safe and affordable shipping across India 🇮🇳

Q: How long does delivery take?

A: Processing takes 1-2 business days, and delivery takes 3-7 business days depending on your location.

A: We accept returns for damaged products or wrong items within 24 hours of delivery. Exchange requests must be raised within 2 days.
